⚡ Quick Start Guide (Under 60 Seconds)

  1. Add Courts & Players: Press P or click ＋ Add Players in the Quick Actions bar. Type comma-separated names, then press + Add.
  2. Pick Match Format: Click 🎯 Format: Best of 1 in the top bar to cycle between Best of 1, 3, or 5.
  3. Generate Pairings: Press R or click 🎯 Round Gen. Select rotation priority (e.g. Balanced games or Longest sitting) and click Generate Round.
  4. Dispatch & Score: On 🏟 Active Courts, tap ▶ Court. Tap + / − directly on the court to score live points.
  5. Complete Sets: When a team reaches winning score with a 2-point gap, tap + Next Set →. Once the match is won, tap ✓ Finish Match & Free Court.
  6. Check Finances & Rankings: Tap 💰 Fees to log payments or 🏆 Ladder to generate championship playoffs.

🏟 Active Courts & Live Scorekeeping Engine Core Engine

The Active Courts module tracks real-time occupancy and allows referees or players to score matches without leaving the facility view.

  • Direct Point Steppers: Each team box features large 38px + and − touch targets plus an editable number field for fast adjustment.
  • Center Set Tracker: The central pill shows the live match set count (e.g., 1 : 0).
  • BWF & ITF Deuce Cap: Automatically flags Deuce (e.g. 20–20 in Badminton) and enforces the 2-point margin up to the hard cap (e.g. 30 in Badminton, 21 in Pickleball, 10 in Tennis).
  • Set Advancement: + Next Set → unlocks only when the active set is decided by official sport rules.
  • Finish & Free: Automatically turns bright green (✓ Finish Match & Free Court) when a side takes the required sets (e.g. 2 sets in Bo3). Frees the court immediately for the next queued match.
  • Early Match Conclusion: Allows concluding early with a confirmation dialog if court time expires or players forfeit.

🎯 Fair Queuing & Player Matchmaking Algorithm

OpenTeamQueue includes smart rotation algorithms to eliminate bench frustration and ensure everyone gets equal court time.

  • Balanced Rotation (Recommended): Prioritizes players with the fewest games played. Minimizes rest inequality across the session.
  • Longest Sitting First: Ensures players who sat out during the previous round are guaranteed court placement in the next round. Leftover players take tracked bench slots.
  • Pure Random: Shuffles players freely for maximum variety and social mixing.
  • Fixed vs Roulette Team Locking:
    • Fixed: Pairs who won or played together in the last round stay united as partners.
    • Roulette: Reshuffles partners every single round so everyone plays with new teammates.
  • Drag-and-Drop Queue Reordering: Grab any row on the Queued Board (or use ▲/▼ nudge buttons) to dynamically change play order. The "Up Next" spotlight follows top priority.

📋 Scoreboard, Tiebreakers & Official Rules Standings

  • Supported Sports:
    • Badminton: 21 points per set, win by 2, hard cap at 30 points (BWF Standard).
    • Pickleball: 11 points per game, win by 2, hard cap at 21 points.
    • Tennis: 6 games per set, win by 2, hard cap at 10 games.
  • Best of 1, 3, 5 Match Lengths:
    • Best of 1: Single set sprint (first to 1 set wins). Ideal for large clubs with high court turnover.
    • Best of 3: Standard match (first to 2 sets wins). Recommended for regular club sessions.
    • Best of 5: Extended match (first to 3 sets wins). Built for tournaments and semifinals/finals.
  • Per-Match Overrides: Click the ⚡ Bo3 ↻ badge on any individual match card to change its format without altering the global session setting!
  • Live Standings Tally: Computes Wins (W), Draws (D), Losses (L), Points For (PF), Points Against (PA), and Games Won/Lost (GW/GL) in real time.

🏆 Championship Round-Robin Ladder Tournament

Seamlessly transition regular open-play sessions into a competitive tournament phase.

  • Automatic Seeding: Reads real player records directly from completed matches and standings.
  • Qualifier Count: Choose Top 4, 6, 8, or 10 players to advance into the playoff bracket.
  • Championship Format Picker: Set championship matches to Best of 3 or Best of 5 independently of regular queue rounds.
  • Round-Robin Grid: Generates all pairwise matchups between qualifiers and places them directly on the Scoreboard for live adjudication.

💰 Financial Accounting & Ledger Accounting

  • Automated Cost Formula: Computes per-player fee based on Shuttlecock Rate + (Court Rental Total ÷ Active Players).
  • One-Tap Check-In: Tap Fee Check-in (F) to toggle paid status as players arrive.
  • Expense Ledger: Record court rentals, shuttlecock tubes, grip tape, or water costs. Tracks Net Balance (Collected vs Spent).
  • CSV Export: One-click export of complete attendee billing records and expenses for club treasurers.
